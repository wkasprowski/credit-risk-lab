How to read this report. Stage 1 covers losses from defaults possible within 12 months; subsequent recoveries can take longer. Stage 2 covers defaults over the remaining lifetime. Stage 3 represents identified default or unlikeliness to pay and uses a modelled LGD loss proxy. Stage 3 PD is set to 100%.
Assumptions. Constant monthly default hazard. Adverse PD odds multiplier: 1.8; LGD multiplier: 1.2. Stage is fixed across scenarios. SICR thresholds and CCF are educational assumptions. This is not a complete IFRS 9 implementation.
